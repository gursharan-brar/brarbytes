# GitHub Game Off 2020, 2021, 2022: themes, rankings, top entries and patterns

**How these notes were gathered (please read before relying on exact numbers).** In this session, direct page fetches of itch.io, github.blog, web.archive.org and plushcap.com were blocked by the session's network policy (DNS/proxy denial), and the shared web-search budget ran out partway through. So every fact below comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages (mostly the itch.io results/rate pages and the GitHub Blog posts), not from my own reading of the full pages. Ranks and scores are quoted as the extracts gave them. Spot-check them on the live pages before publishing anything. To fill the gaps, allow itch.io and github.blog in the session's network settings and re-run, or send a follow-up so the search can continue.

**Rating categories, all three years:** Overall, Gameplay, Graphics, Audio, Innovation, Theme Interpretation. This is stated directly for 2022 ([GitHub Blog 2022 winners](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-winners/)). For 2020 and 2021, the same criteria names appear on the results/rate pages ([2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results); [2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results)).

**URL scheme:** overall results are at `https://itch.io/jam/game-off-YYYY/results`. Per-category pages are `/results/innovation`, `/results/theme-interpretation`, `/results/gameplay` (e.g. [2022 innovation](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results/innovation), [2022 gameplay](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results/gameplay), [2021 theme-interpretation page 19](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results/theme-interpretation?page=19)). Individual entries are `https://itch.io/jam/game-off-YYYY/rate/<id>`.

## Q1. Themes, entry counts, ratings, and the GitHub "winners" posts for each year

### Takeaway
The themes are confirmed: 2020 was **MOONSHOT**, 2021 was **BUG** and 2022 was **CLICHÉ**. Each year drew about 480–560 entries and about 6,300–7,300 ratings. GitHub published a recap each year: a "Highlights" post for 2020, a "Top-rated entries" post for 2021, and a proper "Winners 🏆" post with category winners for 2022. GitHub also tweeted the winners in 2021 and 2022.

### Cited Findings
**2020 – "Moonshot"**
- The theme was "moonshot", and Wikipedia lists 2020 as Game Off IX — [Wikipedia: Game Off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Off); [GitHub Blog: Highlights from Game Off 2020](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/highlights-from-game-off-2020/) (also at https://github.blog/2020-12-23-highlights-from-game-off-2020/).
- GitHub's post says there were "more than 500 tre-moon-dous submissions" — [GitHub Blog: Highlights from Game Off 2020](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/highlights-from-game-off-2020/).
- The results page header reports **6,801 ratings given to 497 entries**, with rating from 2020-12-01 to 2020-12-22 — [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results). Search extracts of the same page also gave "482 entries were submitted" and "479 entries were submitted". This is a conflict I could not resolve. The submitted count probably differs from the rated count because of disqualified or hidden entries (inference).
- A third-party blog gives the jam dates as November 1 to December 1, with about 500 submissions — [learncgames.com](https://learncgames.com/github-gameoff-500-entries/).
- GitHub's recap posts for 2020: "Highlights from Game Off 2020" (dated 2020-12-23 in its URL). Its excerpts feature *Strings of Light*, *Moonarchists* and *Scent of Aurora* — [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/highlights-from-game-off-2020/). A DEV.to post, "GitHub Game Off 🎮 Top picks from 2020 submissions & where to learn game dev", covers the winners. It says *A Trip to the Moon* "took out overall place as well as first place in the Graphics category", and that ParaLunar "won second place overall, and first in the gameplay category" — [DEV Community (GitHub)](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-top-picks-from-2020-submissions-where-to-learn-game-dev-4no0). A follow-up DEV post lists more staff picks, e.g. *Billy's Rocket*, "a challenging physics-based puzzle game built in Godot" — [DEV Community (GitHub)](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-more-staff-picks-twitch-streams-and-get-involved-in-game-dev-2mg8). There is also a highlights video — [YouTube: GitHub Game Off 2020 Highlights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2KRbLyISfdM).

**2021 – "BUG"**
- "The theme for this year's Game Off is… …BUG!" (announced 1 Nov 2021) — [GitHub Blog: Game Off 2021 theme announcement](https://github.blog/2021-11-01-game-off-2021-theme-announcement/).
- The jam ran from 2021-11-01 20:37 to 2021-12-01 21:37 — [itch.io Game Off 2021](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021).
- The results page header reports **6,335 ratings given to 527 entries**, with rating from 2021-12-01 to 2022-01-03. An extract also said "502 entries were submitted", so the two counts conflict — [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).
- GitHub's recap post is "Top-rated entries from Game Off 2021". It presents the 10 highest-rated submissions "as rated and reviewed by the developers that made them", and says "almost 8,000 participants" took the BUG challenge — [GitHub Blog: Top-rated entries from Game Off 2021](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/top-entries-from-game-off-2021/). Entries featured in the post include *Bug on the Moon*, *Use That Gravity Bug*, *Squashy Bug*, *Boutheina: A Bug's Adventure* and *It's Bug Season!* — same source.
- GitHub's tweet: "The #GitHubGameOff 2021 results are in! The overall winner was do;MIN(0) by @anttihaavikko" — [GitHub on X](https://twitter.com/github/status/1478123842916663299).

**2022 – "cliché"**
- The theme announcement defined a cliché as "a phrase or opinion that is overused and betrays a lack of original thought" — [GitHub Blog: Game Off 2022 theme announcement](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-theme-announcement/).
- GitHub called it the "tenth annual" Game Off, with **562 entries**, "7,284 ratings and thousands of hours of playtime" — [GitHub Blog: Game Off 2022 winners 🏆](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-winners/) (also at https://github.blog/2023-01-13-game-off-2022-winners). The itch results header says **7,284 ratings given to 554 entries** — [itch.io 2022 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results). One extract said 550 entries.
- The 2022 winners post names a 1st place for each category: Overall *Neon Square: Resurgence*, Gameplay *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine*, Graphics *Glutton for Punishment*, Audio *Shot In The Dark*, Innovation *Knight In Shining Armour*, and Theme Interpretation *Everything! (but the kitchen sink)* — [GitHub Blog: Game Off 2022 winners](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-winners/).
- GitHub also announced the winners on X — [Overall tweet](https://twitter.com/github/status/1611115661761060864?lang=en), [Gameplay tweet](https://x.com/github/status/1611127058465161218), [Graphics tweet](https://x.com/github/status/1611119402656604160). There are also a [YouTube winners video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7u7Dd6XNZfk), a [DEV post "Top five Godot games and source code from Game Off 2022"](https://dev.to/github/top-five-godot-games-and-source-code-from-game-off-2022-3690) and a Microsoft Learn "The Download" episode mentioning the winners ([link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/the-download/the-download-githubunwrapped-game-off-winners-bflat-for-csharp-and-more)).

**Scoring mechanics**
- itch.io adjusts scores by the median number of ratings per game in the jam, so entries with few ratings can rank differently from their raw scores. This came from a search extract of a Game Off 2020 rate page ([example](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/839543)). It is standard itch.io behaviour, but the statement here rests on that extract.

### Inferences
- GitHub's recaps became more structured over time. 2020 had a highlights and staff-picks style post, 2021 a top-10 with developer reviews, and 2022 an explicit "1st place per category" list. That makes 2022 the easiest year to verify.
- The numbering conflicts (Wikipedia's 2020 = "IX" would make 2022 "XI", but GitHub calls 2022 the "tenth annual") suggest a skipped or renumbered year. This is cosmetic.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full text of any of the three GitHub posts (github.blog is blocked), so I have only excerpts of what they said about each game.
- I could not reconcile the exact submitted vs rated entry counts for 2020 and 2021.

## Q2. Top-ranked entries per year (Overall top 5, plus category leaders), with per-entry details

### Takeaway
I identified the full Overall top 5 for all three years, apart from the author and description of 2021's #3 *Hivemind*. I also have the 1st place in every category for 2022, Gameplay and Graphics winners for 2020, and partial Innovation and Theme lists for 2020 and 2021. Most category #1s for 2020 and 2021 Innovation and Theme are still gaps.

### Cited Findings

#### 2020 (Moonshot): Overall top 5
1. **A Trip to the Moon** by NamraGameDev. Ranked 1st with 64 ratings (score 4.406), #1 Graphics, #2 Audio, #29 Innovation — [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results).
   - What it is: a 2D platformer homage to Georges Méliès' 1902 film *Le Voyage dans la Lune* that "takes you on a journey to the moon to defeat the bad guys" — [DEV/GitHub](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-top-picks-from-2020-submissions-where-to-learn-game-dev-4no0); video and description at [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Az6MgcyW5rU).
   - Art style: a black-and-white silent-cartoon look, with silent-movie intertitles used for the tutorial — [YouTube description via search](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Az6MgcyW5rU).
   - Browser: listed under "Play in browser" in itch collections — [GitHub's Game Off 2020 collection](https://itch.io/c/1630445/github-game-off-2020); [user collection](https://itch.io/c/1863082/gameoff2020).
   - Engine, team size and direct game URL: not found (gap). Only one author is listed (inference: probably solo).
   - Theme: a literal trip to the Moon, made distinctive by its film-homage presentation (inference).
2. **Paralunar** by remruts. Ranked 2nd with 42 ratings (4.381); 1st in Gameplay — [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results); [DEV/GitHub](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-top-picks-from-2020-submissions-where-to-learn-game-dev-4no0).
   - What it is: "a 2D shooting game set inside a haunted castle on the Moon. Shoot, float, dash, and bounce around in low gravity while exorcising ghosts" — [itch.io page](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/838214).
   - Controls: mouse plus keyboard (left-click shoots, right-click or Space dashes, F brakes) — [itch.io page](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar).
   - Engine and platforms: Godot 3.2.3; HTML5, Windows, Linux (browser-playable) — [itch.io page](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar); source at [GitHub repo](https://github.com/Remruts/Paralunar_GameOff).
   - Team: made in a month by Andreas Sturmer, music by Patrick Sturmer (2 people). It is a sequel to their earlier game *Inkorporeal* — [itch.io page](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar).
   - Source conflict: the DEV post calls it a "2D scrolling shooter with cats and random bosses" ([DEV](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-top-picks-from-2020-submissions-where-to-learn-game-dev-4no0)), but the itch page's protagonist is "Alex Gunn: Ghostbuster" ([itch.io](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar)).
   - Theme: the Moon is the setting, and low-gravity movement is the mechanic (inference: literal setting, with the moon expressed as a physics twist).
3. **Launch Party** by plasmastarfish and superduperpacman42. Ranked 3rd with only 13 ratings (4.231); **#2 Innovation (4.615)**; Gameplay #15, Theme #40, Graphics #74, Audio #111 — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/837474); [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results).
   - What it is: a multiplayer ship-programming game **played through a Twitch stream**, suggested for 3+ players — [itch.io page](https://plasmastarfish.itch.io/launch-party).
   - Team: 2 (plasmastarfish did programming, art, music and SFX; superduperpacman42 did programming), all made within the month — [itch.io page](https://plasmastarfish.itch.io/launch-party).
   - Reception: commenters praised the innovative command-programming concept and the music, while some found the controls too hard for casual players — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/837474).
   - Browser vs download and engine: not confirmed (gap).
4. **Moonkind** by Pierre Vandermaesen. Ranked 4th with 31 ratings (4.226) — [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results).
   - What it is: a city-building and management game. The Moon itself is a ship on a 700-year interstellar journey, and you manage the Moonbase to survive — [itch.io page](https://pierre-vandermaesen.itch.io/moonkind); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/820240).
   - Team: built in about a month by a solo indie developer from southern France — [itch.io page](https://pierre-vandermaesen.itch.io/moonkind).
   - Reception: reviewers said the systems were hard to learn because of small text, and that the mid-to-late game was mostly waiting for disasters — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/820240).
   - Theme: a twist, with the Moon reimagined as a generation ship (inference).
   - Browser and engine: not found (gap).
5. **Catapult, cat space program** by Deep-Fold. Ranked 5th with **82 ratings** (4.195); #3 Audio, #3 Gameplay, but #95 Innovation — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447); [itch.io 2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results).
   - What it is: you drag the cat back and release it from a catapult, placing trampolines to help it reach the Moon. It is a fast-paced single-player arcade game in 2D pixel art, made in Godot — [itch.io page](https://deep-fold.itch.io/catapult); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447).
   - Controls: drag-and-release (essentially one mouse gesture) — same sources.
   - Theme: the dev described it as "nothing too complicated, a literal shot to the moon" — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447).
   - Reception: praise for the pixel art, the power-ups, and difficulty that works for both skilled and casual players — same source.
   - Browser: not confirmed for the original. A community Godot adaptation is hosted on GitHub Pages ([repo](https://github.com/jeffreytjs/Catapult-Godot)).

#### 2021 (BUG): Overall top 5
1. **do;MIN(0)** by anttihaavikko. Ranked 1st with 32 ratings (4.562); #1 Gameplay; #3 Innovation; only **#46 Theme Interpretation** — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299219); [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).
   - What it is: a roguelite deck-builder and card puzzle game. You start with a deck of a few simple cards and clear stages by placing them so their pips connect, domino-style. After each stage you add a new card or pick a special **"code injection"** that changes how the game plays — [itch.io page](https://anttihaavikko.itch.io/domino); [game-curator review](https://game-curator.com/jams/game-off-2021-do-min-0/).
   - Tools: made in November 2021 with Unity 2021.1, Inkscape, Krita, GarageBand and Audacity — [itch.io page](https://anttihaavikko.itch.io/domino).
   - Inferences: Inkscape suggests clean vector art. The "code injection" modifiers are the link to the bug theme (a programming or hacking reading of "bug"). Placing cards implies mouse-driven controls. Only one author is listed, so probably solo.
   - Browser vs download: not confirmed (gap). Gameplay video: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cs9zzeaAVxc).
2. **BENDYWORM** by bauxite. Ranked 2nd with only 12 ratings (4.500); **#2 Innovation (4.583)** — [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).
   - What it is: a side-scrolling puzzle-platformer set inside a giant worm, where "the entire world bends and twists around you as you progress through the level" — [itch.io page](https://bauxite.itch.io/bendyworm); [GitHub repo](https://github.com/Bauxitedev/bendyworm).
   - Platforms: **Windows and Linux only** (download) — [itch.io page](https://bauxite.itch.io/bendyworm).
   - Engine: Godot 3.3.3 plus a Rust submodule. Code is MIT and assets are CC BY 4.0 — [GitHub repo](https://github.com/Bauxitedev/bendyworm).
   - Team: the soundtrack is by Kenophos, released separately on Bandcamp — [Bandcamp](https://kenophos.bandcamp.com/album/bendyworm). So at least 2 contributors.
   - Theme: literal (a worm or bug creature), but the innovation comes from a world-bending visual mechanic (inference).
3. **Hivemind**. Ranked 3rd with 14 ratings (4.429) — [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results). Author and description not retrieved (gap).
4. **Doorframe OZ** by ClawBoomGames and 7SevenStudios. Ranked 4th with 37 ratings (4.270) — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299979); [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).
   - What it is: "Stop the evil virus from taking over your 2000's PC!" Made in Unity with C# — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299979); [GitHub Blog 2021](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/top-entries-from-game-off-2021/).
   - A reviewer in GitHub's post called it "a very innovative game with nostalgic appeal" — [GitHub Blog 2021](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/top-entries-from-game-off-2021/).
   - Theme: bug as software bug or virus, framed as nostalgia for 2000s PCs (inference: a non-insect reading).
5. **godot_platformer.exe** by WinRawr. Ranked 5th with 46 ratings (4.261); **#3 Theme Interpretation**; Gameplay #10, Innovation #12, Audio #42, Graphics #106 — [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).
   - Description not retrieved (gap). The `.exe` title suggests a meta "buggy program" platformer (inference only, unverified).
   - Note that its graphics rank was #106, yet it placed 5th overall on gameplay and theme strength.
- 6th overall: *Bug on the moon* — [itch.io 2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results).

#### 2022 (cliché): Overall top 5
1. **Neon Square: Resurgence** by pixelbrain, SundroMusic and ShootDawn (a 3-person credit; the rate page title also lists "Ricardo Palma"). Ranked 1st with 43 ratings (4.395); Gameplay #3, Graphics #8, Innovation #9, Audio #19, **Theme #78** — [itch.io 2022 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1812515).
   - What it is: "A tricky platformer with a twist!" GitHub describes playing as "a neon square as you dash around some incredibly challenging levels" — [itch.io page](https://pixelbrain.itch.io/neon-square-resurgence); [GitHub Blog 2022](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-winners/).
   - Twist: a jam reviewer praised "the idea of disabling some controls", which changes the situation on each level — [itch.io page/comments](https://pixelbrain.itch.io/neon-square-resurgence).
   - Platforms and engine: **HTML5 and Windows**; Unity — [itch.io page](https://pixelbrain.itch.io/neon-square-resurgence).
   - Theme: one extract says the entry used the cliché "Better late than never", but a second search could not confirm this — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1812515). Treat it as unverified.
   - Reception: praise for the graphics, the controls and music that suited the game, along with complaints that it was frustrating and that the first level should have had no obstacles — same sources.
   - Art: neon geometric shapes (inference from the title and description; 2D).
2. **Shot In The Dark** by cavetowerstudios. Ranked 2nd with 47 ratings (4.362); **#1 Audio (4.404)**; Theme #11 (4.298); Innovation #12 (4.043) — [itch.io 2022 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1817029).
   - What it is: you are alone in the dark exploring a mysterious mansion with a very limited light source. Tagline: "When your only light source... is your gun." — [itch.io page](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark).
   - Team: Cave Tower Studios, a two-person team in Los Angeles (Patrick Fye and Matt Tye). Engine: Unity — [itch.io page](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark).
   - Browser: a **WebGL build was added on Dec 07, 2022**, after the Dec 1 submission deadline. The devs say it is the same game, but WebGL lighting was "touchy", and they recommend the download for "sharper graphics, superior lighting, 3D soundscapes, and better support when playing with a controller" — [devlog: Browser Build](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark/devlog/460876/browser-build); [itch.io page](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark). There is also a [bug-fix devlog](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark/devlog/459112/bug-fixes).
   - Inferences: the game is 3D and probably first-person, given "3D soundscapes", lighting, and its listing in a "Browser FPS" collection ([itch collection](https://itch.io/c/4198159/browser-fps)). The idiom "a shot in the dark" is made literal as a mechanic, since your gun is your light.
3. **A Taste Of Their Own Medicine** by AsciiData (also listed under LOKO606). Ranked 3rd with 31 ratings (4.323); **#1 Gameplay**; Innovation #7, Theme #7, Graphics #42, Audio #84 — [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1804176); [itch.io 2022 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results).

GitHub called it "a super hot game with an interesting take on shooting" — [itch.io page](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine); [GitHub tweet](https://x.com/github/status/1611127058465161218).
   - Platform and engine: **Windows only** (download); Unity; source at [GitHub repo](https://github.com/AsciiDataDev/ATasteOfTheirOwnMedicine-GameOff2022) — [itch.io page](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine).
   - Session length: the page lists an average session of "about a half-hour" and tags it Prototype — [itch.io page](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine). The author is credited differently on another page ([LOKO606](https://loko-606.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine)).

The winning recipe was to **make an overused phrase literally true as the core mechanic**, not to parody genre clichés.

- 2022: Innovation #2 and #3 and Theme #2 and #3 are missing, as are the details of *Elephant in the Room*.

## Q3. Browser vs download, 2D vs 3D, solo vs team among top entries

### Takeaway
Among the 15 Overall top-5 entries, **5 are confirmed browser-playable**, **2 are confirmed download-only**, and **8 are unknown**. The confirmed winners are mostly **2D**, with **Godot and Unity** as the dominant engines. A notable exception is 2022, where two of the top 3 (#2 and #3) were 3D first-person Unity games, one of them Windows-only. Teams of 2–3 are common at the very top, but solo entries also reach the top 5.

### Cited Findings
- **Confirmed browser (HTML5/WebGL):** *A Trip to the Moon* ([collection](https://itch.io/c/1630445/github-game-off-2020)), *Paralunar* ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar)), *Neon Square: Resurgence* ([itch](https://pixelbrain.itch.io/neon-square-resurgence)), *Knight In Shining Armour* ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1797048)), and *Shot In The Dark* (WebGL added 7 Dec 2022, after the deadline; [devlog](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark/devlog/460876/browser-build)). The 2022 Theme winner *Everything!* was also HTML5 ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1806716)).
- **Confirmed download-only:** *BENDYWORM* (Windows/Linux, [itch](https://bauxite.itch.io/bendyworm)) and *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine* (Windows, [itch](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine)). The 2022 Graphics winner *Glutton for Punishment* is listed for Windows, macOS and Linux downloads ([itch](https://lehaine.itch.io/glutton-for-punishment)).
- **Engines confirmed:**
  - Godot: *Paralunar*, *Catapult*, *BENDYWORM*, *Knight In Shining Armour* ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar); [itch](https://deep-fold.itch.io/catapult); [repo](https://github.com/Bauxitedev/bendyworm); [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1797048)).
  - Unity: *do;MIN(0)*, *Doorframe OZ*, *Neon Square*, *Shot In The Dark*, *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine*, *Everything!* (sources in Q2).
  - Kotlin LittleKt: *Glutton for Punishment* ([itch](https://lehaine.itch.io/glutton-for-punishment)).
  - None of the entries I could check used Phaser or plain JavaScript.
- **Team size (credited authors):**
  - Two or more: *Paralunar* 2 ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar)), *Launch Party* 2 ([itch](https://plasmastarfish.itch.io/launch-party)), *Doorframe OZ* 2 studios ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299979)), *Neon Square* 3 ([itch](https://pixelbrain.itch.io/neon-square-resurgence)), *Shot In The Dark* 2 ([itch](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark)), *Elephant in the Room* 2 ([results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results)), *Everything!* 2 ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1806716)).
  - Solo: confirmed for *Moonkind* ([itch](https://pierre-vandermaesen.itch.io/moonkind)). Single credited author (so probably solo, by inference) for *A Trip to the Moon*, *Catapult*, *do;MIN(0)*, *godot_platformer.exe* and *Knight In Shining Armour*. *BENDYWORM* has a single code author plus an external composer ([Bandcamp](https://kenophos.bandcamp.com/album/bendyworm)).
- **Rating counts are small:** top entries had 12–82 ratings. *BENDYWORM* placed 2nd with 12 ratings and *Launch Party* 3rd with 13 ([2021 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results); [2020 results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results)). The most-rated top-5 game was *Catapult*, with 82 ratings ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447)).

### Inferences
- 2D vs 3D (inferred from descriptions): 2D for *A Trip to the Moon*, *Paralunar*, *Catapult*, *Moonkind* (management UI), *do;MIN(0)* (cards), *BENDYWORM* (side-scroller), *Neon Square* and *Knight*. 3D for *Shot In The Dark* and *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine*. That makes roughly 8 of the 10 top-5 entries with known descriptions 2D, and 2 3D.
- Download-only did not stop high placement (*BENDYWORM* #2 in 2021, *A Taste* #3 in 2022). But the few high-placing download-only games got very few ratings (12 and 31), while the most-rated top game (*Catapult*, 82) was a quick arcade game. Browser builds probably help collect ratings. This is suggestive, not proven.
- Shot In The Dark's devlog shows that WebGL lighting needed extra tuning work. For a beginner team, this supports staying 2D in a browser-native engine like Phaser.

### Gaps
- Browser or download status for 8 of the 15 top-5 entries.
- Engine for *A Trip to the Moon*, *Launch Party*, *Moonkind*, *Hivemind*, *godot_platformer.exe* and *Elephant in the Room*.

## Q4. How did top Innovation and Theme Interpretation entries interpret the theme: literal or twisted?

### Takeaway
The highest-scoring theme takes were **literal readings turned into a mechanic**, not abstract or meta ideas. In 2022 that meant idioms made physically true. In 2020 it meant the Moon as setting, physics or story. In 2021 the winners split between a software-bug reading and a creature reading. Overall winners often scored poorly on Theme (2021 #1 was #46 on Theme; 2022 #1 was #78), so **theme fit matters most for the Theme category and less for Overall**.

### Cited Findings
- **2022: idioms made literal.**
  - *Knight In Shining Armour* (Innovation #1) turns the shiny armour into a mirror for light-beam puzzles ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1797048); [dhapaak](https://www.dhapaak.com/game/knight-in-shining-armour/)).
  - *Everything! (but the kitchen sink)* (Theme #1) is a pile of idiom microgames, itself a joke on "everything but the kitchen sink" ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-winners/); [itch](https://chroute.itch.io/everything-but-kitchen-sink)).
  - *Shot In The Dark* (Theme #11, Audio #1): "your only light source... is your gun" ([itch](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark)).
  - *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine* (Theme #7, Gameplay #1): "Shoot back while you have no weapons" ([itch](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine)).
  - The theme announcement framed cliché as "a phrase or opinion that is overused" ([GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2022-theme-announcement/)). That definition likely pushed entrants towards phrases rather than genre tropes (inference).
- **2020: Moonshot.**
  - Literal: *Catapult* is "a literal shot to the moon" ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447)), and *A Trip to the Moon* is a Méliès homage ([DEV](https://dev.to/github/github-game-off-top-picks-from-2020-submissions-where-to-learn-game-dev-4no0)).
  - Twists: *Moonkind* turns the Moon into a 700-year interstellar ship ([itch](https://pierre-vandermaesen.itch.io/moonkind)). *Launch Party* (Innovation #2) innovated through its *format*, a Twitch-chat-driven multiplayer ship-programming game ([itch](https://plasmastarfish.itch.io/launch-party)), rather than through its reading of the theme. *Paralunar* uses the Moon for low-gravity movement ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar)).
- **2021: BUG.**
  - Software reading: *do;MIN(0)* uses "code injections" as roguelite modifiers ([itch](https://anttihaavikko.itch.io/domino)), and *Doorframe OZ* has you stopping a virus on a 2000s PC ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299979)).
  - Creature reading with a mechanical twist: *BENDYWORM* (Innovation #2) is set inside a worm whose world bends ([itch](https://bauxite.itch.io/bendyworm)).
  - *godot_platformer.exe* was Theme #3 ([results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/results)); its interpretation was not retrieved.
- **Overall winners with weak Theme ranks:** *do;MIN(0)* was #46 on Theme ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2021/rate/1299219)). *Neon Square* was #78 ([results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/results)). *A Trip to the Moon* was #29 on Innovation ([results](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/results)), and *Catapult* was #95 on Innovation ([rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447)).

### Inferences
- For "SHIFT", the 2022 lesson carries over directly. SHIFT is a word with many concrete meanings: the Shift key, a gear shift, a work night shift, shape-shifting, a paradigm shift, a tectonic shift, redshift, bit-shifting. The pattern that scored on Theme and Innovation was **pick one concrete meaning and make it the core verb of the game**. Two or more layered meanings, as in the kitchen-sink microgame collection, can also win Theme.
- Pure title puns without a mechanic scored low on Theme in 2020 (e.g. "MMOonshot", Theme #241).
- To win Overall, polish and tight gameplay (Gameplay #1–#3 ranks) mattered more than the theme reading. 4 of the 6 Overall #1 and #2 entries were also top 3 in Gameplay.

### Gaps
- The 2020 and 2021 Theme #1 entries, the 2020 and 2021 Innovation #1 entries, and the 2022 Theme #2 and #3, are all unnamed, so I could not show the most surprising interpretations for those slots.

## Q5. Patterns across winners, and postmortems/devlogs

### Takeaway
The top entries were **small-scope, single-mechanic, mostly 2D games** made by 1–3 people in Godot or Unity. Many were browser-playable, and they had a strong presentation hook: the Méliès film look, neon, pixel art, darkness lit by gunfire. I found **very little postmortem material**. Only short itch devlogs surfaced (Shot In The Dark's browser and bug-fix logs, and a later LittleKt update log for Glutton for Punishment). No Reddit threads or YouTube postmortems were retrieved before the search budget ran out.

### Cited Findings
- **Scope signals:**
  - *Knight In Shining Armour* is described as a "short puzzle-platformer" ([dhapaak](https://www.dhapaak.com/game/knight-in-shining-armour/)).
  - *A Taste Of Their Own Medicine* has a listed average session of about half an hour and is tagged "Prototype" ([itch](https://asciidata.itch.io/a-taste-of-their-own-medicine)).
  - *Catapult* is a fast arcade loop of drag, release and trampolines ([itch](https://deep-fold.itch.io/catapult)).
  - *Paralunar* and *Moonkind* were each "made in a month" ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar); [itch](https://pierre-vandermaesen.itch.io/moonkind)).
  - *Paralunar* is a sequel to the team's earlier game *Inkorporeal*, so it reused prior work and experience ([itch](https://remruts.itch.io/paralunar)).
- **Reviewer feedback hints at what raters penalised:**
  - Unclear on-screen controls (*Everything!*, [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1806716)).
  - A hard first level ("first level should have no obstacles", *Neon Square*, [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2022/rate/1812515)).
  - Small text and slow mid-game (*Moonkind*, [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/820240)).
  - Controls too hard for casual players (*Launch Party*, [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/837474)).
  - Praise went to difficulty that suits both skilled and casual players (*Catapult*, [rate page](https://itch.io/jam/game-off-2020/rate/836447)).
- **Devlogs found:**
  - Shot In The Dark's WebGL build post explains the lighting-tuning effort and why the download is the recommended version ([devlog](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark/devlog/460876/browser-build)), plus a bug-fix post ([devlog](https://cavetowerstudios.itch.io/shot-in-the-dark/devlog/459112/bug-fixes)).
  - Glutton for Punishment has a later framework-update post ([devlog](https://lehaine.itch.io/glutton-for-punishment/devlog/675008/update-game-to-use-latest-littlekt-framework)).
  - My search found no devlog for *do;MIN(0)* ([itch](https://anttihaavikko.itch.io/domino)).
- **Developer-written reviews:** GitHub's 2021 post consists of developer-written reviews of the top 10 ([GitHub Blog 2021](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/top-entries-from-game-off-2021/)). Its text was not fully retrieved.
- **Source code:** Game Off requires entries to have source on GitHub, and many winners' repos are public and can be studied.
  - [Paralunar](https://github.com/Remruts/Paralunar_GameOff), [BENDYWORM](https://github.com/Bauxitedev/bendyworm), [A Taste Of Their Own Medicine](https://github.com/AsciiDataDev/ATasteOfTheirOwnMedicine-GameOff2022) and [Glutton for Punishment](https://github.com/LeHaine/ggo2022).
  - GitHub also published a ["Top five Godot games and source code from Game Off 2022"](https://dev.to/github/top-five-godot-games-and-source-code-from-game-off-2022-3690) post.

### Inferences (cross-year patterns for a 3-person beginner Phaser team)
- **Scope:** one core mechanic, a handful of levels or a short loop (roughly 5–30 minutes), and a strong first minute. Raters reward a quick path to fun and punish confusing controls and hard openings.
- **Controls:** the confirmed examples use simple input: mouse aim plus a couple of keys, or a single drag-and-release. Nothing in the evidence suggests complex control schemes win.
- **Art:** a distinctive, cohesive style beats fidelity. Examples are a monochrome silent film, neon squares, and 8-bit-style pixel art (*Glutton for Punishment* won Graphics with pixel art). A beginner team in Phaser can reach this with a constrained palette.
- **2D vs 3D:** mostly 2D. The 3D exceptions came from experienced Unity teams, and one of them struggled with WebGL lighting.
- **Browser:** HTML5 is common among winners, and download-only entries collected fewer ratings. Phaser's native browser output fits this well.
- **Team size:** 2–3-person teams with a dedicated audio person do well on Audio (*Shot In The Dark* Audio #1; *Paralunar*'s music by a second person; *A Trip to the Moon* Audio #2).
- **Theme:** the strongest Theme and Innovation pattern is "take the theme word or phrase literally, then make it the verb of the game". Overall winners still often ranked mid-pack on Theme, so treat theme fit as the multiplier and gameplay polish as the base.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/gamedev, r/indiegaming) threads, YouTube postmortems, or detailed itch devlogs on scope and time spent were retrieved for any top-placing team. The search budget was exhausted before I could look.
- Exact time-to-first-fun and session-length data exists for only one entry.
- Phaser usage among Game Off winners in 2020–2022 is unknown. None of the confirmed winners used it.
