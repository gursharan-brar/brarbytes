# How other game jams handled "Shift"-adjacent themes (2014-2026)

> Method note: in this session, WebFetch was blocked for every jam host I tried (itch.io, gmtk.substack.com, gamemaker.io, goonhammer.com, web.archive.org, and ludumdare.com, which did not resolve). The shared WebSearch budget ran out after about 21 searches. So every "Cited Finding" below comes from search-result summaries of the linked pages, not from reading the full pages. Section 7 (the existing-games catalog) is **background knowledge and was not verified in this session**. It is clearly labelled, and its Wikipedia links are given as pointers to check.

## 1. Which jams used "Shift" (literally) as a theme or name, and what won?

### Takeaway
I found no large, well-known jam whose official theme was the single word "Shift." Game Off 2026 looks like the first big one. Literal "Shift" jams are small: a university jam themed "Shift Happens," won by a color-shifting bullet hell, and some jams that are only *named* SHIFT. Meanwhile, games *titled* "Shift" turn up all the time in jams with other themes. That suggests the word sends people straight to world-switching and character-switching mechanics.

### Cited Findings
- **Game Off 2026 (the target jam):** GitHub's jam is in its 14th year, and the theme is SHIFT. Submissions are due before Dec 1, 2026, 13:37 PST, and voting ends Jan 1, 2027, 13:37 PST. One GitHub page still says "WAVES" in one passage (probably a leftover from the previous year's copy) — [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2026-the-theme-is-shift/); [daily.dev mirror](https://daily.dev/posts/game-off-2026-the-theme-is-shift-3dmkhqjzb). Public repos named like `shift-game-off-2026` already exist — [GitHub example](https://github.com/Anras573/shift-game-off-2026).
- **FI MUNI Gamejam (first edition, about April 2025), theme "Shift Happens":** The winner was **RGBeater** (struhy_xd, timedz351, Lord Of Loneliness, o_sim), "a colorful, color-shifting bullet hell shooter" made in Godot. This is the closest thing to a "Shift"-themed jam winner I found — [RGBeater devlog "Game Jam Winner"](https://struhy-xd.itch.io/rgbeater/devlog/920496/game-jam-winner-).
- **2D SHIFT JAM (March 2025, host 1sl2m):** It used a secret theme revealed at the start, so "Shift" is the jam's name, not necessarily its theme. The page's dates conflict with its rules text — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/2d-shift-jam). There is also a **SHIFT JAM #2** — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/shift-jam-2-).
- **SHIFT GAME JAM (Abu Dhabi Gaming with Unity):** An in-person event series named SHIFT. The October 2026 theme was "Festival of Passports," and the next edition runs **13-15 November 2026**, which overlaps Game Off 2026 — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/shift-game-jam); [mid-east.info](https://mid-east.info/abu-dhabi-gaming-announces-the-next-edition-of-shift-game-jam-13-15-november/).
- **Radial Menu game jam #1 (April 2025):** The winner was "Radial Shift" by Mr_Spirit. The jam's theme was not stated in the results I found — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/radial-menu-game-jam-1).
- **Games titled "Shift" in jams with other themes (shows the word's default associations):**
  - "Shift" by sijilum, GMTK 2022 (theme "Roll of the Dice"): you "shift between versions of the world by rolling dice" — [itch.io](https://sijilum.itch.io/shift).
  - "Shift" by Tall Square Studios, GMTK 2025: a horror game — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2025/rate/3778009).
  - "Shift Shaper" by Ivanything437, GMTK 2021 — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2021/rate/1081525).
  - "Saviors of the Shift," BIGMODE 2023: "forces the player to cycle between 4 different characters with different modes of gameplay" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/bigmode-2023/rate/2424395).
  - "SHIFT" by Peatfire Games, April Fools Game Jam (themes "Tricking" and "Time"), with a devlog titled "How SHIFT fits the game jam theme" — [itch.io](https://peatfire.itch.io/shift); [devlog](https://peatfire.itch.io/shift/devlog/917151/how-shift-fits-the-game-jam-theme).
  - "Spirit Shift," Ludum Dare 30 ("Connected Worlds") — [Code Trip blog](https://codetrip.weebly.com/blog/spirit-shift-a-game-made-in-72-hours-for-the-ludum-dare-30-game-jam).
  - "Shift Core" and "Phase Shift Shadow" appear in the Theme rankings of the Hack & Play: World Jam — [itch.io results](https://itch.io/jam/hack-and-play-world-jam/results/theme).
  - Another "shift" game by winkmoose (no details found) — [itch.io](https://winkmoose.itch.io/shift).
- **Concurrent synonym jam:** Transformation Game Jam 2026 runs in **November 2026** with main theme "Transformation" and optional secondary theme "Hidden." Raters may see near-identical ideas in both jams — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/tf26). The 2023 Transformation Game Jam was "very chill… non-competitive" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/transformation-game-jam). Untitled Game Jam 29 (July/Aug 2020) also used "Transformation" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/untitled-game-jam-29).

### Inferences
- *(Inference)* A small sample of jam games titled "Shift" splits into three readings: (1) toggle between two or more world versions, (2) cycle between characters or modes, (3) horror or "night shift." All three are on GitHub's own example list, so in a 700+ entry Game Off they will likely be the most crowded lanes.
- *(Inference)* RGBeater won a small "Shift Happens" jam with a color-shift mechanic. A color or palette shift can win when the execution is polished (bullet hell plus color), but with 700+ entries it will be common.
- *(Inference)* Concurrent jams with the same theme (Abu Dhabi SHIFT Game Jam, Transformation Game Jam 2026) mean more shift and transform entries on itch.io in November 2026. That makes standing out harder.

### Gaps
- I couldn't find Mini Jam, Godot Wild Jam, Kenney Jam, Pirate Software, GameDev.tv or Weekly Game Jam editions whose theme was "Shift." The one combined search found none, and I couldn't check jam pages directly. Treat this as "not found," not "doesn't exist."
- I couldn't check whether "Shift" has appeared on Ludum Dare theme-voting shortlists (ldjam.com was unreachable).
- I couldn't access full results or entry counts for the FI MUNI "Shift Happens" jam.

## 2. Ludum Dare themes near "shift" (Shapeshift, Connected Worlds, Signal…) and their top games

### Takeaway
LD35 (April 2016) had the theme **"Shapeshift,"** the closest major-jam precedent. It was won overall (48h compo) by **windowframe** by Daniel Linssen. In windowframe, what shapeshifts is the *game window itself*, not a character. That interface-level interpretation is the single strongest "surprising interpretation" precedent for SHIFT. Second place went to The Ooze by Daniel Mullins, who later made Inscryption.

### Cited Findings
- **LD35:** Developers worked "the weekend of April 15th 2016… This year's theme was Shapeshift" — [NIU DCL](https://dcl.niu.edu/gameDev/ludum35.shtml).
- **LD35 #1 Overall (compo): windowframe by Daniel Linssen (managore).** It lets the player reshape the game window to defeat six vampires. Wraithkal's roundup lists it under Fun, Overall and Innovation (compo) — [Wraithkal "Gold Medalists of LD35"](https://www.wraithkal.com/the-gold-medalists-of-ludum-dare-35/); [itch.io page](https://managore.itch.io/windowframe); the developer's announcement, "a game whose window you can manipulate" — [X/Twitter](https://twitter.com/managore/status/721912738021134336). Kill Screen's coverage is titled "A game idea hidden right in front of you" — [Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/a-game-idea-hidden-right-in-front-of-you/). A gameplay video calls it the "overall winner of Ludum Dare 35 [48-hour compo]" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY7NUHn6GQg). The Ludum Dare Explorer lists it at the top for 2016 — [ludum-dare.foon.uk](https://ludum-dare.foon.uk/).
- **LD35 2nd place: The Ooze by Daniel Mullins.** Its itch page says it "Won 2nd place for Overall, Fun, and Mood!" (self-reported, surfaced via the itch.io LD35 + shapeshift tag listing) — [itch.io tag listing](https://itch.io/games/tag-ludum-dare-35/tag-shapeshift).
- **Other LD35 entries (the typical readings):** a platformer where you escape a dungeon by shapeshifting your hero; a short visual novel where you prove a treasure chest is really a mimic; a puzzle game about matching identical shapes — [itch.io LD35 tag pages](https://itch.io/games/tag-ludum-dare-35). Also "Hide and Squeak" by David Strachan — [davetech](http://www.davetech.co.uk/ludumdare35) — and "Shape Shifted" by Simple Potential — [itch.io](https://simplepotential.itch.io/ld35).
- **LD30 "Connected Worlds" (Aug 22-25, 2014):** the closest LD precedent for "two worlds." LudumData lists 2,530 games for the event — [LudumData](https://ludumdata.openfu.com/); entries include "Prismatic Worlds" and many games literally titled "Connected Worlds" — [impe83](https://impe83.itch.io/connected-worlds), [WesleyJohnson](https://wesleyjohnson.itch.io/connected-worlds), [ZwodahS](https://zwodahs.itch.io/connected-worlds), [itch.io LD30 tag](https://itch.io/games/tag-ludum-dare/tag-ludum-dare-30).
- **LD59 (April 17, 2026), theme "Signal":** One team made "a platformer that allows the player to change between four frequencies." Shift-style toggle mechanics appear even under non-shift themes — [Eugene Lee portfolio](https://akapythius.bearblog.dev/games-portfolio); [GitHub topic ludumdare59](https://github.com/topics/ludumdare59).

### Inferences
- *(Inference)* windowframe won because it applied "shapeshift" to the *frame or medium* (the OS window), not the avatar. Players had never seen that, and one simple verb drove the whole game. This is the clearest model for an "unexpected but instantly legible" SHIFT idea. Applying the theme to the UI, the window or the controls beat the shapeshifting-hero entries.
- *(Inference)* Creature or hero shapeshifting was the default LD35 reading (dungeon platformer, mimic visual novel, shape-matching). Expect the same in Game Off, especially since GitHub lists "shapeshifting into creatures" and "shape-shifting aliens" as examples.
- *(Inference)* Many LD30 entries were literally titled "Connected Worlds," and many jam games are titled "Duality" (see §4). Naming the game after the theme is a reliable sign of the obvious interpretation. A team that wants to stand out should avoid "Shift" as a title.
- *(Inference)* For novelty checks on window-manipulation ideas: windowframe (2016) is one prior example; Windowkill (2024, torcado; background knowledge, unverified) is another commercial one.

### Gaps
- I couldn't get official LD35 category rankings beyond #1 and #2, entry counts for LD35, or the LD30 overall winner, because ldjam.com and ludumdare.com were unreachable.
- I couldn't verify top games for other near-theme LDs from the brief (LD41 "Combine 2 incompatible genres," LD50 "Delay the inevitable," LD51 "Every 10 seconds"). The themes are from background knowledge; I found no winners this session.
- The LD58 (Oct 2025) theme was not found.

## 3. GMTK Game Jam themes, "Roles Reversed" (2023), and what Mark Brown said

### Takeaway
The brief's theme list needs a fix: **2022 was "Roll of the Dice"** and **2023 was "Roles Reversed"**. "Roles Reversed" is the closest GMTK theme to SHIFT. Its obvious reading, "play as the boss or enemy," was everywhere: several entries are literally titled "Roles Reversed" or "You Are The Boss." The standouts reversed the role of a *game element or genre* instead. Pause To Play turned the pause menu into platforms, and Mark Brown's favourite, VERSUS SNAKE, turned Snake into first-person horror.

### Cited Findings
- **Theme history (partial verification):** 2017 was "Downwell's Dual Purpose Design" (2,857 creators, 731 games) — [Wikipedia-derived mirror](https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/Game_Maker's_Toolkit); 2022 was "Roll of the Dice" — [sijilum "Shift" page](https://sijilum.itch.io/shift); 2023 was "Roles Reversed," July 7-9, 2023, about 23,100 participants — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Maker%27s_Toolkit) (via search summary); [itch.io jam page](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023); 2025 was "Loop" — [Mark Brown 2025 roundup](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/my-favourite-games-from-gmtk-game); 2026 was **"Count Down,"** with more than 10,500 games and about 37,000+ participants — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/4967-my-favourite-games-from-gmtk-game-jam-2026/); [GameDev.net theme announcement](https://gamedev.net/news/4435-the-gmtk-game-jam-2026-theme-is/). Sources disagree on the 2026 dates (Wikipedia says 22-26 July; another source says August) — [Gaming Companion](https://gamingcompanion.substack.com/p/the-gmtk-game-jam-2026), [Wolfgang Schuster blog](https://wolfgangschuster.wordpress.com/2026/08/05/gmtk-game-jam-2026/).
- **2023 entry count conflict:** Mark Brown's post says 6,800 games; Wikipedia says about 6,900 — [GMTK Substack](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Maker%27s_Toolkit).
- **Mark Brown's process and favourite (2023):** "I played through the top 100 rated games, and picked out my 20 absolute favourites." His favourite was **VERSUS SNAKE** (pizzart, twospaces-sg), which turns "the Nokia phone classic into a legitimately scary first-person horror game" — [GMTK Substack](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam). Its itch page calls it "a 3D analog horror-esque semi-stealth platformer based on the game 'Snake', but with a twist" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2162455). Mark's roundup also describes a game where you play "as the army of enemy skeletons" — [GMTK Substack](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam) (via search summary).
- **Official 2023 winners collection** includes Sneakerdoodle, Pause To Play and Crabjuice (the full list wasn't visible) — [itch.io collection](https://itch.io/c/3491570/gmtk-game-jam-2023-winners).
- **Pause To Play (Ecremprown / Box Penguin Games):** "the role of the pause menu is traditionally to stop the game so you can go get a snack." The game reverses that role: you use the pause menu as platforms to reach the goal. Commenters cite #1 in "Top Marks" and #17 in Creativity, and the dev later calls it their "GMTK 2023 winning game" — [itch.io](https://ecremprown.itch.io/pause-to-play); [Pause To Play Deluxe](https://ecremprown.itch.io/pause-to-play-deluxe); [Box Penguin page](https://boxpenguingames.itch.io/pause-to-play). Skillbox calls it one of the rare jam games that fully uses its unusual concept — [Skillbox Media (RU)](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/smena-roley-12-interesnykh-igr-s-geymdzhema-game-makers-toolkit-2023-goda/).
- **Rhett Pilcher's "How to Win the GMTK Game Jam" (about Pause To Play):** "Don't pick your first idea, maybe not even your second," and choose an idea with a unique twist on the theme — [Medium](https://medium.com/@rhettwpilcher/pause-to-play-how-to-win-the-gmtk-game-jam-b2d53818ec82).
- **Other 2023 Roles Reversed interpretations:**
  - *Meta/designer swaps:* "Making the Game," a platformer where you are the developer — [Skillbox](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/smena-roley-12-interesnykh-igr-s-geymdzhema-game-makers-toolkit-2023-goda/); "Balancer," where you swap roles with the game designer and balance towers and enemies — [itch.io](https://laptopcoder11.itch.io/balancer); "You're Mark Brown" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2157707).
  - *NPC role:* "Someone's Gotta Update the Job Board," where you choose which quests go on the tavern board — [itch.io](https://guybrush20x6.itch.io/someones-gotta-update-the-job-board).
  - *Boss with a twist:* "Hero: Reversed," where you are the boss and dodging raises *player satisfaction* while spamming attacks lowers it, so enemy-AI design becomes the mechanic — [itch.io](https://gjthegamedev.itch.io/hero-reversed); [GitHub](https://github.com/gjtiquia/GMTK-GameJam-2023).
  - *Plain boss/enemy flip (the crowded lane):* "Roles Reversed - But you're the souls boss?" — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2162056); "You Are The Boss" — [itch.io](https://adnan-111.itch.io/you-are-the-boss-gmtk-jam-2023); "BOSSMAN," where you control the enemies — [itch.io](https://andrygast.itch.io/bossman); entries literally titled "Roles Reversed" — [davidjihwan](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2160988), [RebixDev](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2158492), [ham-sandwich47](https://ham-sandwich47.itch.io/roles-reversed).
  - *Pun reading:* "Rolls Reversed," which reverses dice rolls as the core mechanic — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2159191).
  - *Property swap:* "Beswitched," where you swap properties with objects and enemies — [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2154852).
  - *Other:* "Mutiny" — [itch.io](https://brusi.itch.io/mutiny); "Reversal Rooms," where control roles swap — [Skillbox search summary](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/smena-roley-12-interesnykh-igr-s-geymdzhema-game-makers-toolkit-2023-goda/); a "Roles Reversed Cut the Rope" making-of video — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVt7QdEsJbc).
- **Mark Brown on theme interpretation:**
  - 2024 roundup: "it's totally fine for people to interpret the words of the theme in whatever way they like" — [GMTK Substack 2024](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam-500).
  - 2019: he said other jams often use abstract concepts as themes, and he tries to force creators to design games different from the norm — [EGM "Jam Sesh"](https://egmnow.com/jam-sesh-how-a-popular-online-contest-sparks-gameplay-innovation/).
  - Q&A: "start small… [don't] think their first game is going to be as big and as complicated as Grand Theft Auto V!" — [Medium Q&A](https://medium.com/hackathon-entertainment/gmtk-game-jam-q-a-with-mark-brown-72b157961472).
  - 2025 ("Loop") roundup: he highlights Pac-Man-style wraparound and repeating a level for mastery, and says "the more restrictions you put on the player, the more devious they must become" — [GMTK Substack 2025](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/my-favourite-games-from-gmtk-game).
- **GMTK 2026 "Count Down" favourites** include Picture Day, 11:59, Circuit Breaker, Game Jam Simulator 2026, Some Reassembly Required, Veggie Brawl and Count Your Clones — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/4967-my-favourite-games-from-gmtk-game-jam-2026/); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awegilW3DTc).

### Inferences
- *(Inference)* The winning Roles Reversed pattern: **reverse the role of a familiar game element** (pause menu, Snake, the job board, the boss's goal) rather than "you are the bad guy." For SHIFT, the parallel is to shift something players *don't expect to move*: the UI, the window, the save system, a genre's camera, a classic game's premise. Compare windowframe (§2) and Pause To Play.
- *(Inference)* Rhett Pilcher's article is titled after Pause To Play, so he is probably its developer. That makes "don't pick your first idea, maybe not even your second" advice from a GMTK top-ranked winner.
- *(Inference)* VERSUS SNAKE shows the "**familiar classic + genre or perspective shift**" formula: take a game everyone knows and shift its genre (arcade to horror) or camera (top-down to first-person). This scales down well for beginners, because the base rules are already understood.
- *(Inference)* Brown avoids abstract-word themes on purpose (EGM). "Shift" is exactly that kind of abstract word, which pushes most entrants to the literal and dictionary readings. That is why the GitHub example list will be saturated.

### Gaps
- I couldn't access the full text of Mark Brown's 2023 top-20 post or video transcript (Substack fetch blocked; amara.org subtitle files exist but weren't fetched), so I have **no direct Brown quote listing over-used Roles Reversed ideas**. The boss-flip saturation claim rests on the many same-named entries above, not on a Brown statement.
- 2018-2021 and 2024 themes ("Genre"?, "Only One"/"Just One," "Out of Control," "Joined Together," "Built to Scale") are from background knowledge or unclear search snippets. One search summary mentioned an undated Brown contest themed "Just One." I couldn't confirm them against the Wikipedia table.
- I couldn't retrieve top-ranked placements for 2020 "Out of Control" or 2021 "Joined Together."

## 4. Global Game Jam themes near shift ("Duality" 2022) and standout entries

### Takeaway
GGJ 2022 "Duality" is the closest GGJ precedent. Site-level winners clustered around mirror, polarity, black/white worlds and two-character control. The more distinctive ones paired the duality with a second constraint, such as solving two puzzles at once or a duel across dimensions. The brief's later GGJ themes (2023 "Roots," 2025 "Bubble," 2026) could not be verified in this session.

### Cited Findings
- **GGJ 2022 theme "Duality"** — [GGJ 2022 games listing](https://v3.globalgamejam.org/2022/games); [GGJ YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q14zYjPmtec).
- **NYU Game Center site:** Best Overall was *Re: Live* (Silverjay Studio); Best Use of Theme: Duality was *Everyday Fantasy* (Shibas); Audience Choice was *Vitality* — [NYU Game Center](https://gamecenter.nyu.edu/global-game-jam-2022-award-winners/).
- **Media Design School (Auckland):** 17 games. Highlights were *Duelity* (a Robot and a Cowboy duel through a wormhole between their dimensions) and *Abyss of Kings* (solve two puzzles at once while guiding both characters) — [Media Design School](https://www.mediadesignschool.com/news/global-game-jam-2022-wraps).
- **UAT (Arizona):** *Mirror Block* won Best Design, *Harm Charm* Best Art, and *Polarity* Best Technical — [UAT](https://www.uattech.com/global-gam-jam-2022-productivity-through-duality).
- **Other Duality entries:** "Duality" by 56 Hearts, a puzzle platformer about reuniting "with your friends and your foes"; another entry where the player alternates between white and black worlds — [GGJ 2022 game page](https://v3.globalgamejam.org/2022/games/duality-1-0); [itch "Duality"](https://jspenaranda.itch.io/duality).
- **Other GGJ themes (verified bits):** 2018 was "Transmission" — [GGJ](https://v3.globalgamejam.org/theme-2018); 2021 was "Lost and Found"; 2024 was "Make me laugh"; a "Repair" year is mentioned without a date (2020 per background knowledge). Themes are set by a Theme Committee — [GGJ history](https://globalgamejam.org/history).

### Inferences
- *(Inference)* For "Duality," the crowded readings were polarity, mirror, black/white world-swap and two-character control. Titles like *Duality*, *Duelity*, *Polarity* and *Mirror Block* show it. GitHub's "shifting between two overlapping worlds" example is the same lane, so expect it to be the single most common Game Off 2026 mechanic.
- *(Inference)* The standouts did *two things at once*: simultaneous two-puzzle control (Abyss of Kings), or a duel *across* dimensions (Duelity). A world-switch idea needs a second twist to stand out.

### Gaps
- GGJ 2023 ("Roots"), 2025 ("Bubble") and 2026 themes were **not confirmed** by search; the globalgamejam.org history page wasn't readable.
- There is no global GGJ ranking (GGJ is non-competitive at the global level); the winners above are per-site awards.

## 6. Patterns: overused vs surprising interpretations (what separates creative entries from the first idea)

### Takeaway
In every jam above, the losing pattern is "**theme word → the most literal mechanic → title the game after the theme**." The winning pattern is "**apply the theme to something players assume is fixed**": the window (windowframe, LD35 #1), the pause menu (Pause To Play, GMTK 2023 winners collection), a classic game's genre or camera (VERSUS SNAKE, Brown's #1), or the boss's *goal* rather than its identity (Hero: Reversed).

### Cited Findings
- **Overused or crowded readings seen in shift-adjacent jams (with evidence):**
  1. *Toggle between two worlds or versions* — sijilum "Shift" [itch](https://sijilum.itch.io/shift); black/white world-swap in GGJ 2022 [GGJ](https://v3.globalgamejam.org/2022/games/duality-1-0); many LD30 "Connected Worlds" entries [itch tag](https://itch.io/games/tag-ludum-dare/tag-ludum-dare-30); "Phase Shift Shadow" [itch](https://itch.io/jam/hack-and-play-world-jam/results/theme). This is also on GitHub's own example list — [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2026-the-theme-is-shift/).
  2. *Shapeshifting hero or creature* — LD35 dungeon shapeshifter platformer, mimic VN, "Shape Shifted," "Hide and Squeak" [itch LD35 tag](https://itch.io/games/tag-ludum-dare-35), [Simple Potential](https://simplepotential.itch.io/ld35), [davetech](http://www.davetech.co.uk/ludumdare35).
  3. *Cycle between characters or modes* — "Saviors of the Shift" [itch](https://itch.io/jam/bigmode-2023/rate/2424395); two-character control in GGJ 2022 [Media Design School](https://www.mediadesignschool.com/news/global-game-jam-2022-wraps).
  4. *Play as the boss or enemy (role flip)* — multiple GMTK 2023 entries titled "Roles Reversed" or "You Are The Boss" [davidjihwan](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2160988), [Adnan_111](https://adnan-111.itch.io/you-are-the-boss-gmtk-jam-2023), [BOSSMAN](https://andrygast.itch.io/bossman).
  5. *Color, polarity or palette shift* — RGBeater (won the small "Shift Happens" jam) [devlog](https://struhy-xd.itch.io/rgbeater/devlog/920496/game-jam-winner-); *Polarity* and *Mirror Block* at GGJ 2022 [UAT](https://www.uattech.com/global-gam-jam-2022-productivity-through-duality).
  6. *Puns on the theme word* — "Rolls Reversed" [itch](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2159191). For SHIFT, the pun lanes are the GitHub list items: Shift key, stick shift, work shift, bitwise shift.
  7. *Night-shift horror* — "Shift" (GMTK 2025, horror) [itch](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2025/rate/3778009); also GitHub's "night-shift convenience-store horror" example [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/open-source/gaming/game-off-2026-the-theme-is-shift/).
- **Surprising or standout interpretations that placed well (with links):**
  - **windowframe** (LD35 #1 Overall compo): shapeshift the *game window* — [itch](https://managore.itch.io/windowframe), [Wraithkal](https://www.wraithkal.com/the-gold-medalists-of-ludum-dare-35/).
  - **Pause To Play** (GMTK 2023 winners collection): the pause menu's role is reversed; it becomes platforms — [itch](https://ecremprown.itch.io/pause-to-play), [winners collection](https://itch.io/c/3491570/gmtk-game-jam-2023-winners).
  - **VERSUS SNAKE** (Mark Brown's #1 favourite of GMTK 2023): Snake becomes first-person analog horror — [GMTK Substack](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam), [itch](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2162455).
  - **Hero: Reversed** (GMTK 2023): as the boss, you maximise the *hero's fun*, not their death — [itch](https://gjthegamedev.itch.io/hero-reversed).
  - **Making the Game / Balancer / You're Mark Brown** (GMTK 2023): the player becomes the developer, designer or critic — [Skillbox](https://skillbox.ru/media/gamedev/smena-roley-12-interesnykh-igr-s-geymdzhema-game-makers-toolkit-2023-goda/), [Balancer](https://laptopcoder11.itch.io/balancer), [You're Mark Brown](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2157707).
  - **Someone's Gotta Update the Job Board** (GMTK 2023): you play the NPC who writes the quests — [itch](https://guybrush20x6.itch.io/someones-gotta-update-the-job-board).
  - **Abyss of Kings** (GGJ 2022): two puzzles solved simultaneously — [Media Design School](https://www.mediadesignschool.com/news/global-game-jam-2022-wraps).
  - **Beswitched** (GMTK 2023): swap *properties* (not places) with objects and enemies — [itch](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2023/rate/2154852).
- **Explicit advice on "first idea":** "Don't pick your first idea, maybe not even your second" — [Rhett Pilcher, Medium](https://medium.com/@rhettwpilcher/pause-to-play-how-to-win-the-gmtk-game-jam-b2d53818ec82). Brown says interpretations can be loose — [GMTK 2024](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/the-best-games-from-gmtk-game-jam-500) — and that restrictions make players "more devious" — [GMTK 2025](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/my-favourite-games-from-gmtk-game).

### Inferences
- *(Inference)* Ways to pick an angle that recur among the standouts:
  - (a) **Shift the frame, not the content.** Window, UI, pause or menu screen, save file, camera genre, the credits.
  - (b) **Shift whose goal it is.** Hero: Reversed, Job Board: the player serves another agent's experience.
  - (c) **Shift a classic everyone knows.** VERSUS SNAKE: borrowing familiar rules saves tutorial time, which matters for beginners.
  - (d) **Shift a property, not a position.** Beswitched.
  - (e) **Run two states simultaneously** instead of toggling. Abyss of Kings.
- *(Inference)* Concretely, for SHIFT: candidate "frame-level" shifts not seen in the evidence above include shifting the *game's own text or keyboard layout*, the *aspect ratio or window* (but windowframe and Windowkill exist), *which player-input device controls which thing*, and the *time-of-day of the player's real clock*. These are my brainstorm, not observed jam entries. Check them against §7 first.
- *(Inference)* Small, crisp, single-verb games won: windowframe, Pause To Play. This matches Brown's "start small" advice and suits a 3-beginner team.

### Gaps
- I have no quantitative counts of interpretations per jam (e.g., "X% of Roles Reversed entries were boss-flips"); the over-use list is qualitative, based on the visible sample and repeated titles.
- I found no Mark Brown quote explicitly about "the first idea everyone has."

## 7. Existing notable games built on "shift" mechanics (novelty-check catalog)

### Takeaway
Most "shift" mechanics a beginner team might pitch already have well-known commercial precedents: gravity flip, world toggle, 2D/3D perspective shift, polarity or color, time shift, possession or body swap, shift-work sims and night-shift horror. Precedents don't disqualify an idea in a jam, but the more famous the precedent (VVVVVV, Fez, Papers, Please, FNaF), the more raters will notice. **Everything in this section is background knowledge and was NOT verified in this session.** The Wikipedia links are pointers for the team to confirm.

### Cited Findings
- (Session-verified examples only) **windowframe** (2016), a game whose window you manipulate — [itch.io](https://managore.itch.io/windowframe). **Pause To Play** (2023), the pause menu as a platform — [itch.io](https://ecremprown.itch.io/pause-to-play).
- All other titles below are background knowledge; see Inferences.
